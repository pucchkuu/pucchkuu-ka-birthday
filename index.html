Our little story ❤️

This one is different. This is the part I really wanted you to read.

I don't really know when you became such an important part of my life. Maybe it happened somewhere between all those coaching days, our random conversations, the stupid things we used to laugh about, and all those ordinary moments that somehow became some of my favourite memories. I just know that somewhere along the way, you stopped being just someone I knew and became someone who slowly became a part of my everyday life, my comfort, my happiness, and my little world.

And then came 8 May 2024 — the day we became us. ❤️

Since then, we've collected so many little moments together. The school days, the laughing, the stupid fights, the making up, the promises, the understanding, the endless conversations, and all those tiny moments that probably didn't look like much to anyone else but meant everything to me. Especially those little lunch-break moments when you'd come just to see me for a few seconds. Maybe it was only a few seconds, but somehow those few seconds were enough to make my entire day better.

When I think about us, I don't only remember the big moments. I remember the tiny ones. The random updates. The silly conversations. The way we could talk about absolutely nothing and still not want the conversation to end. The comfort of knowing there was someone I could tell things to. The feeling of having my person somewhere in my day.

We've never been perfect, and I don't think I would want our story to be perfect anyway.

We've fought. We've misunderstood each other. We've said things we didn't mean properly. We've had days when we didn't know how to understand what the other person was feeling. There have been moments when things became difficult enough that we almost forgot how much good existed between us.

But somewhere through all of that, there was always something that brought us back to understanding each other.

Maybe that's what makes our story special to me.

We didn't just have the easy days.

We learned each other through the difficult ones too.

We learned that loving someone isn't only about saying the right things when everything is perfect. Sometimes it's about being patient when the other person is scared. Sometimes it's about listening when you don't completely understand. Sometimes it's about giving each other space without making that space feel like distance. And sometimes it's simply about choosing to try again.

I think we've both changed along the way.

Not necessarily into the people we were before, but into people who understand each other a little better now.

And maybe that's what this new chapter of us is about — not trying to recreate everything exactly as it was, but building something even more honest, comfortable and beautiful from everything we've learned.

Now we're long-distance, and honestly, I miss all those little things more than I probably say. I miss having you close. I miss those random moments when seeing you was just a normal part of my day. I miss the little things that I never thought I'd have to miss.

But somehow, even with all this distance between us, you're still such a big part of my everyday life.

And I don't know exactly what our future will look like.

I don't know what life will bring us, how much we'll change, where we'll end up, or what our story will look like years from now.

But I know one thing.

I want to keep discovering it with you.

I want more random conversations, more stupid jokes, more memories, more annoying each other, more laughing until we can't breathe, more little moments that seem insignificant at the time but become precious later.

I want to keep growing with you.

I want to understand you better, and I want you to understand me better too. I want us to keep learning how to love each other in the ways we actually need, rather than trying to be some perfect version of a couple.

Because maybe that's what I love most about us.

We're not a perfect story.

We're just us.

A little messy. A little stupid. Sometimes complicated. Sometimes ridiculously happy. Sometimes difficult. But full of memories, comfort, effort, laughter, growth and so much love.

And if someone asked me what my favourite part of our story is, I don't think I'd choose one particular day.

I'd choose the fact that somewhere between two ordinary people simply talking, laughing and spending time together, we became something that mattered this much.

And if I had to choose again, knowing everything we've been through, knowing the easy days and the difficult ones, knowing how much we've grown and how much we still have to learn...

I'd still choose you.

Not because our story has always been easy.

But because it's ours.

And because, somehow, through all the chapters, you're still the person I look at and think,

"I'm really glad it was you."
